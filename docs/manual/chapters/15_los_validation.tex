\chapter{Level of service and validation}
PySig reports a named LOS standard and delay basis. The default 0.4.5
classification is explicitly a queueing-delay screening measure. It is not
formal HCM control-delay LOS unless the required control-delay components are
implemented.

Mathematical validation should use hand-calculated queue cases and published
traffic-flow relationships. Ak\c{c}elik, Besley and Roper (1999),
\textit{Fundamental Relationships for Traffic Flows at Signalised
Intersections}, ARR 340, provides Australian empirical context for queue
discharge headways, saturation flow, start loss, end gain, jam spacing,
queue-clearance waves and downstream interference.
